Questions people ask

What if I'm not tech-savvy?

Gone is designed specifically for non-technical people. The setup is step by step, and we use simple, clear language throughout. If you can send an email, you can use Gone — and support is there when you want a hand.

Can I update my information anytime?

Yes. You can add, edit, or remove anything at any time. We actively encourage keeping it current, and can send optional reminders every few months to review what you've stored.

When can my family access my information?

You stay in control of when and how your trusted contacts get access. Some information can be available immediately — medical details, for instance — while other items stay private until they're genuinely needed.

Do I still need a lawyer?

Gone complements traditional estate planning rather than replacing it. For legally binding documents like wills and trusts, we recommend speaking with an estate planning attorney. Think of Gone as the practical companion to that work.

What stage is Gone at?

Gone is in early development, and we're talking to people about what would actually be useful before we build further. If this is something you'd want, we'd like to hear from you — those conversations shape what gets made.
